Everything Casync runs, entry by entry.

Seventeen service lines across finance, compliance, people, operations and technology. Where a filing needs professional certification, we prepare the evidence and hand it to your CA, CS or lawyer directly — no separate portal needed.

FINANCE OPERATIONS

01
Accounting Operations

Bookkeeping, ledger posting, bank and vendor reconciliation, fixed asset accounting, month-end and year-end close — delivered as clean data ready for Tally, Zoho Books, BUSY or your ERP of choice.

02
GST & Tax Data Operations

Sales and purchase registers, ITC datasets, GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B working papers, e-invoice and e-way bill support. Casync prepares the data — your CA reviews and files.

03
Income Tax & Tax-Audit Support

Ledger extracts, depreciation and loan schedules, TDS information and audit evidence compiled and organised ahead of your tax-audit deadlines.

04
Accounts Payable

Purchase order through to payment: three-way matching, approval routing and payment proposal batching, so nothing gets paid twice or missed.

05
Accounts Receivable

Invoicing, customer ledgers, ageing reports and collection follow-ups — so overdue receivables get a number and an owner, not just a mention in the next meeting.

08
Compliance Evidence Management

Every filing backed by an organised evidence pack — registers, reconciliations, exceptions and explanations — marked "ready for professional review" before it reaches your CA.

About CaSync

CaSync exists to make accounting and financial-market stories readable — the kind of content that usually stays locked behind technical language, written instead for anyone who wants to actually understand what's happening in the numbers.

We publish two ongoing series: India, covering GST, Ind AS, company law and Indian market stories, and Global, covering IFRS, US GAAP and financial-market accounting stories from around the world.

Alongside the writing, CaSync runs a free Ask an Expert service. Questions submitted through the site are routed to a panel of independent professional Chartered Accountants (CA), Company Secretaries (CS), CPAs and IFRS specialists, who respond directly by email. CaSync connects you to these professionals — the answers themselves come from the responding expert, who is responsible for the advice given.

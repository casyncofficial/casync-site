Privacy Policy

Last updated: [add date when you publish]

What we collect

When you submit a question through Ask an Expert, we collect the information you provide: your name, mobile number, email address, question category, and the text of your question.

Why we collect it

  • To route your question to an appropriate expert on our panel
  • To allow the responding expert to reply to you directly by email
  • To contact you if we need to clarify your question

We do not use this information for marketing without your separate consent, and we do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Who sees your information

Your submission is received at our official inbox (casyncofficial@gmail.com) and shared with the specific expert on our panel who answers your question. We take reasonable steps to keep this information confidential.

How long we keep it

We retain question submissions only as long as needed to respond to you and maintain a basic record, and delete or anonymize data on request.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. To exercise these rights, email casyncofficial@gmail.com.

Consent

By checking the consent box on the Ask an Expert form, you agree to this policy for the purpose of having your question answered.

This policy is a starting draft written to cover the Ask an Expert form specifically. Have it reviewed by a lawyer before you rely on it for full DPDP Act compliance, especially as the site grows or adds analytics/advertising.

OUR ENGAGEMENT MODEL

A single, accountable operations partner — not another vendor to manage.

Most growing businesses assemble their finance and compliance function piecemeal — an in-house bookkeeper, a CA engaged once a quarter, a payroll spreadsheet nobody fully trusts. The gaps between those pieces are where deadlines slip and numbers go unreconciled. Casync closes those gaps: we act as the operating layer that gathers, reconciles and organises your financial and compliance data to a professional standard, then routes it to your chartered accountant, company secretary or legal counsel for review and certification, exactly where their sign-off is required.

ONE — DISCOVERY

We map your operations

A structured review of your accounting, compliance and reporting as they stand today, and where they're falling short.

TWO — DELIVERY

We run the operating engine

Bookkeeping, reconciliations, GST and payroll data, procurement workflows and reporting, delivered against an agreed monthly calendar.

THREE — CERTIFICATION

Your professionals sign off

We hand your CA, CS or legal counsel a complete, reconciled evidence pack — review takes minutes, not a week of chasing paperwork.
